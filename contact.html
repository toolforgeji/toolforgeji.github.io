Get in Touch

Have an idea for a new client-side tool? Encountered an issue? Send us feedback. Since ToolForge has zero backend telemetry, your direct input is how we improve!

Prefer email? Write directly to hello@toolforge.dev

Before you send a message

Tell us what you need, and the details that help us act on it. Tool requests work best when you describe the task, the input you start with, and the output you expect.

  1. Request a tool: describe the job it should do and an example input and output.
  2. Report a bug: include your browser and version, device or OS, the steps that cause the problem, and the tool page URL.
  3. General feedback: share what worked, what felt slow or confusing, and what you would change.

Please do not paste personal, financial, or confidential data into this form. ToolForge runs in your browser and has no backend telemetry, so your messages are one of the main ways we learn what needs fixing.
