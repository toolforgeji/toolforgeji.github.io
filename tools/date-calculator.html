Frequently Asked Questions

How are business days calculated?
Business days count only Monday through Friday, automatically filtering out weekend days (Saturday and Sunday).
Can I add or subtract months and leap years?
Yes! The date math engine respects varying month lengths (28, 29, 30, and 31 days) as well as leap year February boundaries.
Does countdown work when minimized?
Yes, modern browsers keep timers running in background tabs.

About the Date & Time Calculator

Calculate the days between two dates, count business days while excluding weekends, add or subtract durations, and set live countdowns. Useful for project plans, contract deadlines, and personal milestones. Calculations run locally in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Pick a start and end date, or enter a duration.
  2. Choose the calculation type.
  3. Read the result, including business-day totals.

How to use the Date & Time Calculator

  1. Choose a tab: Days Between Dates, Add / Subtract, or Countdown.
  2. Enter your start and end dates, or a base date with an amount and unit.
  3. Turn on Include end day if you want the end date counted in the total.
  4. Read the elapsed days, business days, or target date in the result panel.
