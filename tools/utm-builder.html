Frequently Asked Questions

What are UTM parameters?
UTM parameters are five query tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content) appended to a URL that tell Google Analytics exactly where traffic originated.
Why should I use lowercase for UTM parameters?
Google Analytics is case-sensitive. Using 'Email' and 'email' splits traffic into two separate channels. Lowercase keeps reporting consolidated.
Can I combine UTMs with QR codes?
Yes! Copy the generated UTM URL and paste it into our QR Code Generator tool to track scans on physical flyers and banners.
