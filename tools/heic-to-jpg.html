How ToolForge Converts HEIC Photos

01

Local HEIF Parsing

The binary bitstream of Apple's High Efficiency Image Format is ingested locally.

02

WebAssembly Transcoding

A compiled libde265 / libheif Wasm runtime unpacks the YCbCr video frames into RGB pixels.

03

Direct JPEG Export

The unpacked bitmap is encoded into standard JPEG and saved to your device.

Zero Server Ingestion

Your private family photos, selfies, and vacation memories are never uploaded to the cloud. All decoding happens inside your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't Windows or Android open HEIC files?
HEIC is Apple's high-efficiency image container based on HEVC video compression. Converting to standard JPEG makes your photos viewable on all platforms.
Are my iPhone photos uploaded anywhere?
No. The heic2any decoder runs directly in your browser memory via WebAssembly.
Will I lose image detail or color vibrancy?
We encode at 90%+ JPEG quality by default, ensuring full dynamic range and sharp clarity.
