Frequently Asked Questions

Does image compression happen on your servers?
No. All canvas drawing, quantization, and WebP/JPEG re-encoding takes place in your local browser thread.
Which format gives the smallest file size?
WebP typically achieves 25% to 35% smaller file sizes than standard JPEG at equivalent visual quality.
Can I resize dimensions at the same time?
Yes, choose from presets like 1920px (Full HD), 1280px (HD), or 800px in the Max Dimension dropdown.
