Frequently Asked Questions

How does two-way unit conversion work?
Modifying either the left or right input immediately recalculates the corresponding unit in real time.
Are binary digital storage prefixes (1024 bytes) used?
Yes, digital units convert using standard binary multiples (1 KB = 1024 Bytes, 1 MB = 1024 KB).
How are temperature conversions handled?
Temperature utilizes precise thermodynamic equations: Fahrenheit (°F) = (°C × 9/5) + 32, and Kelvin (K) = °C + 273.15.
