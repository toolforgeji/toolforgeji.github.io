How ToolForge Compresses Your PDF

01

Client-Side Parsing

Your PDF file is read directly into your browser's ArrayBuffer memory via the HTML5 File API.

02

Stream Optimization

Redundant font descriptors, duplicate xref tables, and unused object streams are stripped and compressed.

03

Instant Local Save

The optimized PDF bytes are compiled into a Blob object for direct browser download. No cloud roundtrips.

Your PDF Never Leaves Your Device

Unlike conventional PDF compression websites that transmit your contracts, tax returns, and medical records to remote cloud servers, ToolForge runs entirely inside your web browser. You can even disconnect your internet before pressing compress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server for compression?
No. The PDF is parsed and compressed in your browser's local memory using WebAssembly. Your files are never uploaded.
Will the text in my PDF become blurry?
Vector text and embedded typography remain crisp and clear. Compression optimizes internal streams, removes duplicate objects, and strips unused metadata.
Is there a limit on PDF file size?
You can compress PDFs as large as your device's available RAM can handle, typically up to 200MB+ without issues.
