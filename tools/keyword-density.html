Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ideal keyword density for SEO?
Most SEO professionals recommend keeping primary keyword density between 1.0% and 2.5%. Anything exceeding 3.0% risks algorithmic keyword stuffing penalties.
What are 2-word and 3-word phrases?
These are bi-grams and tri-grams—consecutive sequences of two or three words (e.g. 'client-side tools', 'free utility suite') that mirror long-tail search intent.
Should I remove common words (stopwords)?
Yes, stopwords (the, a, and, of) appear frequently in natural English without adding search intent. Filtering them uncovers meaningful topical keywords.
