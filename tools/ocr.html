Frequently Asked Questions

How does client-side OCR work without an API key?
Tesseract.js compiles the native C++ Tesseract OCR engine directly into WebAssembly, running machine learning optical models inside your browser thread.
What languages are supported?
We currently bundle trained LSTM models for English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and Portuguese.
What types of images give the best accuracy?
High-contrast documents with clean lighting and straight text lines produce near 100% accuracy.
