How ToolForge Removes Backgrounds

01

Corner & Edge Sampling

The outer edge pixels and perimeter chroma are sampled to calculate the dominant backdrop color space.

02

Delta-E Alpha Masking

Per-pixel Euclidean color distance and edge luminance equations calculate transparent alpha levels.

03

Feathering & Export

Edges are smoothed and anti-aliased, and the final cutout is exported as a crisp 32-bit PNG.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded to a cloud server?
No. The pixel color segmentation and alpha masking execute entirely on your device's GPU and CPU via HTML5 Canvas.
Can I replace the background with a solid color instead of transparent?
Yes! You can choose transparent, solid white, dark mode, or pick any custom hex color.
Which photos work best?
Images with a reasonably distinct subject (e.g. products against plain walls, signatures on white paper, portraits, logos) yield the cleanest cutouts.
