Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between camelCase and PascalCase?
camelCase starts with a lowercase letter (e.g. myVariableName), while PascalCase starts with an uppercase letter (e.g. MyClassName).
What does Slugify do?
Slugify converts headlines and titles into clean, URL-friendly strings with lowercase words separated by hyphens (e.g. 'my-first-blog-post').
Does it handle accented letters and umlauts?
Yes, our slug generator normalizes diacritics (e.g. 'é' becomes 'e') to ensure pure ASCII URL compliance.
