Understanding the EMI Formula

Reducing Balance Method

Interest is recalculated every month against the remaining principal balance, lowering interest costs over time.

Standard Formula

EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n - 1], where r is annual rate / 12 and n is tenure in months.

Tenure vs Interest Tradeoff

A longer tenure lowers your monthly payment but significantly inflates total interest paid over the life of the loan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the formula for calculating loan EMI?
EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n - 1], where P is Principal loan amount, r is monthly interest rate, and n is duration in months.
Can this calculate home loans, auto loans, and personal loans?
Yes, any reducing-balance amortized loan (home mortgage, car loan, student loan, personal financing) uses this exact mathematical model.
How does extra principal prepayment affect the schedule?
Prepaying principal directly shrinks the balance, compound interest, and total months required to retire the debt.
