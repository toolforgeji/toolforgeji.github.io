Frequently Asked Questions

Where should robots.txt and sitemap.xml be placed?
Both files must be placed directly at the root level of your domain (e.g. https://yourdomain.com/robots.txt and https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml).
Does robots.txt hide pages from appearing in Google?
Disallow in robots.txt tells search bots not to crawl the page, but the URL can still be indexed if linked elsewhere. For complete de-indexing, use a 'noindex' meta tag.
Can I submit sitemap.xml to Google Search Console?
Yes, once uploaded to your root folder, submit your sitemap URL under the Sitemaps tab in Google Search Console to speed up page discovery.
