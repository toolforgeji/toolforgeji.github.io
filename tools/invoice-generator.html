Frequently Asked Questions

Can I save or print the invoice as a PDF?
Yes! Clicking 'Print / Save as PDF' triggers your browser's native print engine with custom @media print rules that render a clean, high-resolution document.
How do I change the currency symbol?
Select your currency from the Currency dropdown (supporting $, €, £, ₹, CAD, and AUD).
Can I add custom line items and tax?
Yes, click '+ Add Item' to add as many deliverables as required. Taxes and discounts are calculated automatically.
