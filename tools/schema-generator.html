Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Google prefer JSON-LD for structured data?
Google officially recommends JSON-LD because it lives inside a standalone script block without polluting your user-facing HTML markup.
Where do I paste this JSON-LD snippet?
Paste the entire <script type="application/ld+json"> block inside your page's <head> or <body> tags.
How can I test the generated schema?
You can paste the generated code into Google's official Rich Results Test (search.google.com/test/rich-results).
