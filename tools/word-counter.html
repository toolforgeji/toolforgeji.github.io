Frequently Asked Questions

How is reading time calculated?
Reading time is calculated using the globally accepted average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute (WPM). Speaking time uses 130 WPM.
What is the Flesch Reading Ease score?
It rates text on a 100-point scale. Scores between 60 and 70 are considered standard readable English accessible to 8th and 9th graders.
Does this count hyphens as separate words?
Hyphenated words like 'well-known' are counted as single lexical units according to standard modern linguistic guidelines.
