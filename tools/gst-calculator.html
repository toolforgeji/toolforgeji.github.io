GST Formula Reference

Exclusive GST

GST = (Amount × Rate) / 100
Total = Amount + GST

Inclusive GST

Net = (Amount × 100) / (100 + Rate)
GST = Amount - Net

Intra vs Inter-State

Intra-state transactions split 50/50 between CGST and SGST, while inter-state applies 100% IGST.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between GST Inclusive and Exclusive?
GST Exclusive adds tax on top of your base price (Total = Base + Tax). GST Inclusive extracts the tax portion already built into the final gross retail price.
How are CGST and SGST calculated?
For intra-state commerce, the total GST rate is divided equally between Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST).
Can this calculator be used for international VAT?
Yes! The inclusive and exclusive formulas apply identically to UK VAT (20%), European VAT, Australian GST (10%), or Canadian HST.
