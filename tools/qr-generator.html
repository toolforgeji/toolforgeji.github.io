Frequently Asked Questions

Do these QR codes ever expire?
Never. These are static, direct QR codes that contain your exact data with no redirection middleware or trackable URLs.
How do Wi-Fi QR codes work?
When scanned on an iPhone or Android camera, the operating system recognizes the standard WIFI: protocol and prompts the user to join the network with one tap.
What is Error Correction Level?
Error correction adds redundancy. Level H allows up to 30% of the code to be covered, obscured, or damaged while still scanning cleanly.
