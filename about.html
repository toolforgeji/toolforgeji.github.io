The ToolForge Manifesto: Privacy Without Compromise

Every day, millions of people upload confidential tax documents, proprietary source code, patient records, and private family photos to free online utility websites. What most users don’t realize is that these files are stored on remote servers, logged in databases, or used for data harvesting.

Our Core Promise

ToolForge was built on an uncompromising principle: your data should never leave your machine. We leverage the full power of modern WebAssembly (Wasm), client-side Canvas APIs, Web Workers, and contemporary browsers to perform document manipulation, optical character recognition, image compression, and calculations completely offline.

How It Works: Zero-Backend Architecture

When you use ToolForge, your browser downloads a static bundle containing HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. From that moment forward:

  • PDF Processing: Handled by pdf-lib running in WebAssembly/JS memory. Pages are parsed and re-rendered entirely within browser heap space.
  • OCR (Optical Character Recognition): Executed by Tesseract.js, compiling neural network models directly via WebAssembly.
  • Image Manipulation: Done through the HTML5 2D Canvas context and OffscreenCanvas threads.
  • Financial & Developer Math: Computed instantaneously via native JavaScript engines (V8, JavaScriptCore, SpiderMonkey).

You can test this yourself: open any tool on ToolForge, turn on Airplane Mode or disconnect your Wi-Fi, and the tools continue to work flawlessly.

Zero-Upload Privacy Policy

We do not operate backend ingestion servers. We do not use third-party user-session trackers. We do not maintain user accounts or require cookies for core tools.

The only data stored is your local theme preference (dark/light) in your browser’s own localStorage.

Open & Freely Deployable

ToolForge is published under the permissive MIT License. You can clone the repository, run it on GitHub Pages, or self-host it on an air-gapped corporate intranet for internal security teams.
